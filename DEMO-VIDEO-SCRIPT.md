## Part A — record any time (no keys needed)

### 0:00–0:20 — The problem (talk to camera, or voiceover on the homepage)
> "Small pet services lose real money to no-shows. A customer books a
> sitter, the sitter blocks the time, turns down other work — then the
> customer just doesn't show. And booking by scattered texts makes it
> worse, because details get lost."

On screen: the app homepage / services list.

### 0:20–1:00 — AI scheduling assistant (screen recording)
Type this exact request into the assistant:
> "watch my dog Milo next Friday evening"

Say while it fills in:
> "The customer just types it in plain English. The AI turns it into a
> booking — service, date, and time land in the form by themselves. No AI
> key needed, it works with a built-in parser."

On screen: the request going in, then the booking form pre-filled
(service, date, time window).

### 1:00–1:20 — Pick the service, show the deposit math
> "Dog Walking is $25 a walk. The deposit is 30 percent — $7.50 — and the
> app figures that out on the server, not in the browser."

On screen: service list with demo prices (Dog Walking $25 / $7.50 deposit,
Pet Sitting $60 / $18.00, Grooming $55 / $16.50, Boarding $45 / $13.50,
Drop-In Visits $20 / $6.00). Say once: "These are demo prices."

## Part B — record only after the sandbox payment test passes

Needs: PayPal sandbox keys in, Render demo live, sandbox personal account
ready to log in at checkout.

### 1:20–2:05 — PayPal deposit (screen recording)
> "A booking isn't confirmed until the 30 percent deposit is paid through
> PayPal. A customer who has paid shows up."

On screen: fill in pet and owner details, pay the deposit with the PayPal
sandbox personal account. Say once: "Sandbox only — no real money moves."

### 2:05–2:30 — Confirmation (screen recording, then close)
> "After PayPal confirms it, the app writes a plain confirmation — booking
> details, deposit paid, and the PayPal order ID — so both sides have the
> same record."

On screen: confirmation screen with booking, payment status, AI-written
summary, PayPal order ID. Close with one line:
> "RavFia Pet Services — book it, pay the deposit, no more no-shows."
